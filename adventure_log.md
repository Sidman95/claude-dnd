## Session 0 — Campaign Setup (no play)

Star Wars Clone Wars campaign initiated. System: D&D 5e SRD reskinned for Star Wars (see `system_sw5e.md`).

**Player character:** Sid — human Jedi Padawan, 19, gifted in both lightsaber and the Force. Padawan to Master Tann Lorrik. Built his lightsaber on Ilum at twelve; green crystal. Force-Sensitive feat (Variant Human).

**Starting setup:**
- Aboard the *Resolute Tide* en route to Vorlag, a Mid Rim agriworld that went silent six days ago.
- 327th Clone Battalion ("Stonewalls") embarked.
- Master Lorrik commands the mission. Captain Reez commands the clones.

**Background tension:**
- The Force feels *wrong*, and most Jedi sense it without knowing why.
- Sid has been hearing an unfamiliar voice in his meditations. He has told no one.

Session 1 begins on the cruiser, the morning of arrival at Vorlag.
