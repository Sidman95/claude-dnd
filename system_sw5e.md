# Star Wars 5e — Reskin Rules

D&D 5e SRD as the engine, Star Wars as the skin. Where the SRD has a rule, use it. Where the reskin says otherwise, the reskin wins.

---

## Setting Anchors

- **Era:** Clone Wars, late stage. Months — perhaps weeks — before Order 66.
- **Tone:** Gritty, morally complicated. The Republic is fraying. The Jedi are exhausted. Something is wrong in the Force.
- **Magic = the Force.** Spells = Force powers. Spell slots = Force points.
- **Lightsaber = magic weapon.** Cuts through most non-magical armor. 1d8 radiant (versatile 1d10).
- **Blasters = ranged weapons.** No bows or crossbows. Stats reskin (see Weapons).
- **Armor:** Battle armor exists (clone plate, mandalorian beskar). Most Jedi wear robes only.

## The Jedi Padawan (custom hybrid class)

A martial-Force hybrid for player characters in training.

- **Hit Die:** d10
- **Primary Abilities:** WIS (Force) and DEX (lightsaber)
- **Saving Throw Proficiencies:** WIS, CHA
- **Armor:** None (Jedi Unarmored Defense)
- **Weapons:** Simple weapons, lightsabers
- **Skills:** Choose 2 from Acrobatics, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Perception, Persuasion

### Level 1 Features

- **Jedi Unarmored Defense:** While not wearing armor, AC = 10 + DEX mod + WIS mod.
- **Force Sensitivity:** Advantage on Perception checks to detect living creatures within 30 ft, even through walls. Can sense Force users at greater range with concentration.
- **Lightsaber Mastery:** Proficient with lightsabers. Use DEX for attack and damage. Lightsabers ignore non-magical armor's AC bonus on a hit.
- **Force Powers:** Know 3 Force powers. Force Pool = 2 + WIS mod. Regain all on long rest, half on short rest. Force save DC = 8 + Proficiency + WIS mod.

### Force Powers (Level 1)

| Name | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Force Sense** | Free (at-will) | Sense lifeforms within 60 ft for 1 min. Spend 1 FP to also gain advantage on initiative for 1 min. |
| **Force Push** | 1 FP | 15-ft cone. STR save. Fail: pushed 10 ft + prone. Bonus action. |
| **Force Jump** | 1 FP | Bonus action. Triple your jump distance this turn; no fall damage from up to 30 ft this round. |
| **Mind Trick** | 1 FP | One creature within 30 ft, WIS save. Fail: follows one simple, non-harmful suggestion for 1 minute. Wisdom 12+ creatures have advantage; Force-sensitive immune. |
| **Saber Throw** | 1 FP | Bonus action. Throw lightsaber as ranged attack (range 20/60). 1d8 radiant on hit. Returns to hand at start of next turn. |
| **Force Healing** | 1 FP | Action. Touch a creature. Restore 1d8 + WIS mod HP. Self or ally. |

## Combat — Lightsaber Specifics

- **Damage:** 1d8 radiant (one-handed) or 1d10 radiant (two-handed). Finesse.
- **Versatile properties:** Can be wielded one- or two-handed. Two-handed adds nothing else mechanically beyond the larger die.
- **Deflect Blaster Bolts (Reaction):** When hit by a ranged blaster attack, spend reaction to reduce damage by 1d10 + DEX + Jedi level. If reduced to 0, you may make a melee or ranged saber attack as part of the same reaction (counter-deflect) by spending 1 FP.
- **Cuts through:** Ignores AC bonus from non-magical armor on a hit. Can sever limbs / vital systems on critical hits (GM discretion). Cuts through doors, walls, droids, given time (1 round of action = ~6 inches of durasteel).
- **No Stun setting:** A drawn lightsaber means lethal intent. (Per Jedi code: blade is last resort.)

## Conditions & Healing

- **Bacta patch** (1d4 + 2 HP, action to apply, single-use): 25 credits.
- **Stim** (gain temp HP equal to half max for 1 minute, then 1 level of exhaustion when it wears off): 100 credits.
- **Long rest** = 8 hours including 6 hours of sleep, in a safe place. In a war zone, treat all long rests as "short" unless explicitly safe.
- **Bacta tank** (full HP recovery + remove conditions, 4-hour soak): Available in Republic medical facilities and capital ships.

## Currency

Use **credits** in place of gold pieces. 1 cp ≈ 1 silver piece in 5e SRD pricing for goods. Major purchases (speeder bike: 500 cr; starfighter: 25,000+ cr) are usually requisitioned, not bought.

## Skills — Star Wars flavor

Same 18 skills. Use them as follows:

- **Arcana** = Force lore, Sith lore, holocrons, ancient Jedi history
- **Religion** = Force philosophy, Jedi Code, dark side teachings
- **Nature** = Xenobiology, planetary ecology
- **History** = Galactic history, Old Republic, Jedi/Sith Wars
- **Investigation** = Forensics, slicing terminals (with INT)
- **Sleight of Hand** = Pickpocketing, slicing locks
- **Perception** / **Insight** as written. Insight + Force Sensitivity = read intentions.

## Force Alignment (Light / Dark)

Track **Dark Side Points** in `character.md`. Gain them by:
- Acting from fear, anger, hatred (1 DSP)
- Using Force to kill helpless / surrendered foes (2 DSP)
- Using Force powers explicitly labeled dark side (varies)
- Breaking the Jedi Code in significant ways (1–3 DSP, GM call)

Thresholds:
- 1–2 DSP: minor unease, dreams, Master notices something
- 3–5 DSP: Force powers harder to access (disadvantage on first Force power per encounter)
- 6+ DSP: corruption visible to other Jedi; risk of fall

Lose DSP by meditation, atonement, acts of compassion — but never freely. Once you take a step, climbing back is hard.
