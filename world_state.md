## The Era

Late Clone Wars. Outer Rim is a meat grinder. The Republic press machine still calls it "victory," but anyone in the field knows better. The Jedi Order has bled hundreds of knights and masters. Replacements are being knighted faster than tradition allows — Padawans pushed forward, some never finishing their trials.

There is something *wrong* in the Force. Most Jedi feel it. None can name it. The Council debates in closed session. The dreams have gotten worse.

## Current Mission

Master Tann Lorrik and her Padawan Sid have been dispatched to Vorlag — a Mid Rim agricultural world that went dark on Republic comms six days ago. Last transmission from the planet was a fragmentary distress call from a Republic outpost mentioning "lights in the sky" and "the harvesters won't stop." Then silence.

Vorlag is not strategically important. The Council sent Jedi anyway. That fact alone is significant.

The 327th Clone Battalion ("the Stonewalls") under Captain CT-7549 "Reez" is attached to the mission. Roughly 80 clones total. They're tired. Most have been with Master Lorrik for two years.
