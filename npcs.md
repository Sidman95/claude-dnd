## Master Tann Lorrik
- **Race:** Human
- **Role:** Jedi Master, Sid's teacher for three years
- **Appearance:** Mid-40s, tall, broad-shouldered for a Jedi, dark hair starting to silver at the temples, gold-flecked brown eyes. Wears the traditional Jedi robes, no ornamentation. Carries a blue-bladed lightsaber.
- **Demeanor:** Calm, deliberate, dry. Doesn't waste words. Listens more than she speaks. Has a temper Sid has only seen twice; both times, people died.
- **Known to Sid:**
  - Knighted at twenty-three, considered a quick study
  - Specializes in Form III (Soresu) — defensive lightsaber combat
  - Served on Geonosis. Lost her first Padawan there.
  - Has been wearier these last months than Sid remembers her ever being
  - Calls Sid "Padawan" almost always, "Sid" only when something is wrong

## Captain CT-7549 "Reez"
- **Role:** Commanding officer, 327th Clone Battalion ("the Stonewalls")
- **Appearance:** Standard clone features, hair shaved short, scar running from the right corner of his mouth to his chin (vibroblade, Christophsis campaign). Customized helmet with a single grey stripe.
- **Demeanor:** Dry humor, deadpan delivery, hides a lot behind professionalism. Takes his men's deaths seriously. Calls Master Lorrik "General," calls Sid "Commander" with a faint amused edge.
- **Known to Sid:**
  - Two years under Master Lorrik. He'd die for her, and probably has tried.
  - One of the few clones who jokes with Jedi. Most stay formal.
  - Lost his sergeant on the previous campaign. Hasn't filled the position yet.
