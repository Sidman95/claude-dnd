## 1. VORLAG TRUTH (THE PRIMARY MYSTERY — DECIDED)

**THE TRUTH:** Beneath Vorlag's grain-belt lies a **Sith wayshrine** — not a holocron, not a temple, but an *anchor stone* from the Old Sith Empire, buried two millennia ago when the world was a battleground in a forgotten war. The colonists never knew. The shrine has been dormant — *until the harvesters dug too deep.*

Six standard months ago, the **Halrick Combine** (the agri-conglomerate that runs Vorlag's harvest fleet) authorized deep-soil tilling rigs on the southern continent to open new growth fields. One of those rigs — Harvester Unit **HC-Forty-Two** — punched through the cap-stone of the shrine in the township of **Brennfeld**. It did not stop. It cannot stop. The shrine bleeds dark side influence through any organic mind in proximity, and the harvester crews were the first to drink it.

**How it spreads — the timeline:**
- **Month 6 ago:** Harvester HC-42 cracks the cap. No one notices. Crew has nightmares.
- **Month 5:** HC-42 crew refuses to leave the field. Other harvesters drift toward Brennfeld.
- **Month 3:** Brennfeld's town council stops sending tax reports. The Combine sends an auditor. The auditor stays.
- **Month 2:** Surrounding townships report missing grain shipments. People who travel to Brennfeld do not return — or return *changed*, quiet, working the fields with a stillness that's wrong.
- **Month 1:** Planetary Governor **Veska Tonn** declares a quarantine "for blight." It is not blight. She is trying to contain it. She may already be losing.
- **Week 1:** Comm relay sabotaged from inside. Distress call leaks out — "lights in the sky" (the shrine pulses at night), "the harvesters won't stop" (literal — the rigs run 24/7, crewed by thralls).
- **Now:** Roughly **one-third** of the southern continent's population is in the shrine's pull. They are not zombies. They are *quietly devoted.* They will smile at strangers, offer them food, and at night drag them to the pit.

**Who knows what:**
- **Governor Veska Tonn:** Knows there's a pit, knows people are wrong, does NOT know it's Sith. Thinks it's a parasite or a chem leak. Quarantined herself in the capital, Tessach.
- **Foreman Joren Halrick** (Combine site chief at Brennfeld): Fully turned. Welcomes new arrivals. Believes he serves "the deep song."
- **The colonists in the north:** Confused, terrified, blaming the Combine, blaming the Republic.
- **The Council:** Knows nothing. Suspects Separatist activity. Sent Lorrik because she's expendable and competent — they've already written her off (this is the Council's quiet shame).
- **The Voice:** Knows EVERYTHING about Vorlag. The shrine is part of its plan.

**What the players find at each stage:**
- **Stage 1 (Orbit / Approach):** Sensors show no Separatist signature. No fleet. No droids. Just a planet that's gone quiet. Lorrik feels the wrongness immediately. Sid feels it deeper — and the Voice is *louder* here.
- **Stage 2 (Tessach, capital):** Governor Tonn alive, paranoid, blocking access. Refugee camps. People who escaped Brennfeld are catatonic or screaming. One survivor, **Mira Kell**, can describe the pit if pressed (see NPCs).
- **Stage 3 (Travel south):** Empty townships. Harvesters running with no crew visible (the crews sleep in the fields now). Ambush by turned colonists who will *not* talk, only smile and reach.
- **Stage 4 (Brennfeld):** The town is a cathedral of stillness. Foreman Halrick offers them tea. The pit is a wound in the earth, ringed by HC-42's torn-up tracks. Pulsing red glow at the bottom.
- **Stage 5 (The Shrine):** Descent. The Voice becomes *clear* for Sid here — it speaks his name. The shrine is a black basalt obelisk inscribed with Sith script. Lorrik recognizes it from the archives and is visibly shaken.

**The climax of the arc:** The shrine cannot be destroyed by Sid at level 1 — it is millennia old and rooted. But it CAN be **re-sealed.** The cap-stone, broken by the harvester, lies in pieces. A ritual seal (Force-light meditation, two Jedi, sustained for minutes while turned colonists attack) will dampen the shrine for another generation. Success = Vorlag saved, but a wound on Sid's mind. Failure = the shrine wakes, and something old starts dreaming.

The **Voice will tempt Sid** during the climax: *Don't seal it. Touch it. Learn what your masters fear.* This is his first real dark-side check.

## 2. THE VOICE

**Identity (decided):** The Voice is **Darth Sidious**, Force-probing through a Sith ritual he's been refining for years to identify and groom Force-prodigies before the Order falls. He does not yet know Sid's name or location with precision — Sid is one of perhaps a dozen distant resonances he's tracking. The proximity to the Vorlag shrine will **sharpen the connection dramatically.** After Vorlag, Sidious will know him.

The Voice does not announce itself as Sidious. It speaks in impressions, half-sentences, *invitations*. Examples:
- "*You feel it too. The wrongness. They lie to you about it.*"
- "*Your master is afraid of you. Have you noticed?*"
- "*There is more. They have shown you so little.*"

After the Vorlag shrine, the Voice will be able to speak full sentences and offer specific gifts (a Force technique, a warning that saves Sid's life, etc.). Each gift accepted = +1 Dark Side Point.

**The long game:** Sidious wants Sid as a backup apprentice — a discarded option if Anakin fails or if Vader is later lost. Sid is *Plan G,* but Sidious cultivates all his options patiently.

## 3. MASTER TANN LORRIK — HIDDEN LAYER

- **Loyalty to Council:** 4/10. She lost faith on Geonosis when they told her Pell's death (her first Padawan) was "an acceptable cost." She has stayed because of Sid and because leaving means abandoning him to someone worse.
- **Trust in Sid:** 8/10. Genuine love, mentor-deep. But weighted by fear.
- **The Secret:** Three weeks ago, during meditation, Lorrik had a **vision of Sid in black robes**, lightsaber red, standing over her body. She has told no one. Not the Council, not Sid. She has been watching him for signs ever since. The weariness Sid has noticed is *this.* She is trying to figure out whether to trust the vision, fight it, or run with him somewhere the Council can't reach.
- **Hidden tension:** If Sid uses dark-tinged Force at any point in front of her, her trust drops by 2 immediately. If she sees him resist the Voice, it climbs.
- **The Geonosis wound:** She wields Soresu because she watched Pell die in a Djem So flourish that left him open. She blames herself. Pell's saber crystal is in a pouch on her belt. Sid does not know this.
- **Soft trigger:** If Lorrik dies before the Vorlag climax, Sid is alone with the Voice. This is a darker branch but not the planned one.

## 4. CAPTAIN REEZ — CHIP STATUS

When Order 66 comes, Pip will hesitate for one beat — and that beat is enough for Sid to act, IF Sid is paying attention to people like Pip. Reward for treating clones as men.

## 5. ORDER 66 CLOCK

**Not triggered.** Fires when narratively right. The Vorlag arc must resolve first — players need attachments worth burning.

**Three candidate triggers:**
1. **End of Vorlag arc, en route to next assignment** — the *Resolute Tide* receives the encoded transmission while in hyperspace. Sid is meditating. Lorrik is on the bridge. Reez is at her side. Classic, brutal, immediate.
2. **Middle of Arc 2** (see below) — Sid has built a relationship with a non-clone ally, and Order 66 hits while the party is split. Maximum chaos.
3. **At a moment of triumph** — Sid has just done something heroic and the Voice has been quiet for a while. He has let his guard down. Then the comms crackle.

**Preferred trigger:** #1. It's clean, it's iconic, it gives Sid one full arc with his Master before he loses her. Use #2 or #3 only if the game drifts toward them.

When it fires: Lorrik dies UNLESS Sid acts immediately AND Reez's chip saves throw succeeds. Default assumption: Lorrik dies. This is the inciting wound of the rest of the campaign.

## 6. NPC ROSTER (DM-ONLY)

### Clones (327th "Stonewalls")

- **Sergeant Hatch (CT-9911):** Tall for a clone, scar across his jaw from a vibroblade on Felucia. Loves bad caf, worse jokes. Loyal to Lorrik. **Secret:** Has been keeping a list of every brother in the 327th who died under her command. Two hundred and eleven names. He shows it to no one. He's not bitter — he just wants to remember. **Chip stable.** Wants Sid to grow up fast.

- **Trooper Pip (CT-2256):** Younger-faced (a quirk of growth acceleration), stutter, paints lothcats on his pauldron. **Secret:** He's writing a memoir. Hides the datapad in his bunk. It's terrible and earnest. **Chip stable, but hesitates.** Wants Sid to think he's brave.

- **Medic "Stitch" (CT-4408):** Quiet, deft hands, missing two fingers on his off hand. **Secret:** He's done unauthorized chip diagnostics on himself and Reez. He KNOWS something is wrong but doesn't know what the chips are. He thinks it's a tumor cluster — a Republic medical conspiracy to control aggression. He's right and wrong. **Chip glitching like Reez's.** Wants Sid to know that not all of them will survive what's coming, even if he can't say why.

### Vorlag NPCs

- **Governor Veska Tonn:** Mid-50s, silver hair, sleep-deprived, wears a sidearm she's never fired. **Motivation:** Save her world from a contagion she doesn't understand. **Secret:** Her son was in Brennfeld. She has not heard from him in five weeks. She knows. She has not admitted it to herself. **What she wants from Sid:** Permission to firebomb the southern continent from orbit. She will ask. She means it.

- **Mira Kell:** Twenty-something, harvester mechanic, escaped Brennfeld on foot through eighty kilometers of grain-fields. Burnt, half-mad, alive. **Secret:** She's not fully clean. There's a thread of the shrine in her still. Sometimes she smiles at nothing. If the players bring her with them, she will betray them at the worst possible moment — quietly, almost apologetically. **What she wants from Sid:** To be told it's over. She'll lie to herself that it is.

- **Foreman Joren Halrick:** Forties, broad, sun-weathered, smiling. **Motivation:** Welcome them to the deep song. **Secret:** He's the high priest now, though he wouldn't use the word. He genuinely believes he is at peace. He is the local antagonist of the arc but not its mind. **What he wants from Sid:** For Sid to listen to the pit, just for a moment. Just to hear.

### Shadow Antagonist

- **The Voice / Darth Sidious:** Already detailed in section 2. Not on Vorlag in person. Will not be on-screen for arcs to come. But his presence in Sid's head is the throughline of the entire campaign.

## 7. PLANNED ENCOUNTERS (LEVEL 1 PACING)

Per CLAUDE.md: start CR 1/4 to 1/2. No instant-TPK threats. Sid is a Padawan with a saber but also fragile.

### Encounter 1: "The Empty Town" — Tessach Outskirts (Investigation, no combat)
- Abandoned farmstead, signs of struggle, a child's toy in the dirt, datapad with last entries in growing handwriting that loops the same phrase: "the harvest is good." DC 12 Investigation: find a hidden cellar with a survivor (terrified, useful info).
- **Learn:** Something is wrong, people are not themselves, the south is the source.

### Encounter 2: "Smiling Welcome" — Township of Vren (Social → optional combat, CR 1/4 each)
- Four turned colonists (use **Commoner** stat block, AC 10, HP 4, but with **Force-touched** trait: advantage on saves vs being charmed/frightened, +1 to hit). They will not attack first. They invite the party to stay. If pressed or attacked, they swarm. Four CR 1/4 = manageable for Sid + Lorrik + 2 clones.
- **Avoidance option:** Wisdom (Insight) DC 13 lets Sid feel the wrongness in them and slip past. Lorrik can suggest leaving — let Sid choose.
- **Learn:** This is not Separatist. It is Force. Lorrik will say it aloud if pressed: "*This is dark side.*"

### Encounter 3: "The Field at Night" — Approach to Brennfeld (Stealth / chase, CR 1/2)
- Two **Field Thralls** (use **Cultist** stat block, CR 1/8, HP 9, scimitar = vibroscythe, +3 to hit, 1d6+1) plus a **Harvester rig** rumbling through the field — environmental hazard, not an enemy. If spotted, the rig's lights swing toward them. Stealth check DC 14 group, or run (DEX saves to avoid the harvester's blades, 2d6 slash on fail).
- **Avoidance option:** Stealth past entirely. Reward = arrive at Brennfeld unseen.
- **Learn:** The harvesters are tools, not enemies. The thralls protect them.

### Encounter 4: "Tea with the Foreman" — Brennfeld town hall (Social, escalating)
- Halrick offers tea and conversation. He will not attack. He wants Sid to *listen.* The Voice rises here. DC 15 Wisdom save for Sid to keep it muted. On fail, the Voice speaks a full sentence in his head for the first time: "*He's not wrong, you know.*" This is the dark-side temptation beat.
- **No combat unless players start it.** If they do: Halrick (use **Cult Fanatic**, CR 2 — STRONG, but the party has Lorrik and clones to absorb) plus 4 thralls.
- **Learn:** The pit. Its location. What it does to those who listen.

### Encounter 5: "The Sealing" — The Shrine (Climactic, mixed)
- Descent into the pit (~30 meters). Black basalt obelisk. The Voice is loud, *personal,* uses Sid's name. Lorrik begins the seal ritual — she needs Sid to hold the second pole of the light. While they channel: **6 thralls** attack in waves of 2 (CR 1/8 each, manageable). The clones hold the line. The ritual takes 4 rounds. Sid CAN break off to fight; if he does, the ritual fails.
- **Dark-side check:** During round 3, the Voice offers Sid a specific gift — *the location of the Sith holocron buried below the obelisk.* Accept = +2 Dark Side Points and a permanent narrative hook. Refuse = +1 Light Side flavor, the Voice goes quiet for several sessions.
- **Learn:** What the dark side feels like from the inside. That his Master is afraid of him (she will glance at him during the ritual, and he will see it).

## 8. BRANCHING PATHS

- **Sid succeeds at Vorlag (seal holds):** The shrine dampens. Vorlag begins to recover, slowly. The Council praises Lorrik. Lorrik's vision of Sid in black does NOT come true today — her trust in him rises (+2). The Voice goes quiet for two sessions. Then Order 66.

- **Sid fails / seal breaks:** The shrine fully wakes. A dark-side beacon now blazes from Vorlag, visible to any Force-user in the sector. Sidious's attention sharpens *dramatically* — the Voice now knows Sid's name. Vorlag is lost; Republic orbital-bombards. Lorrik survives but is broken by it. Order 66 fires earlier, harsher.

- **Sid retreats from Vorlag:** Lorrik will not let him — unless Sid insists, in which case she goes alone. If she goes alone, she dies sealing it. Sid carries that. The Voice will use it. Hard path. Order 66 fires soon after, and Sid is alone for it.

- **Master Lorrik dies (any cause):** Sid inherits her saber AND Pell's crystal (in the pouch he didn't know about). The Council will assign a new Master — but the campaign pivots: Sid is now functionally unaccompanied, the Voice escalates immediately to full sentences, and Arc 2 becomes a fugitive arc whether Order 66 has fired or not.

## 9. CAMPAIGN ARC STRUCTURE (LONG-TERM)

- **Arc 1 — Vorlag (now):** The shrine. Establish the Voice. Establish Lorrik. Establish the 327th. Levels 1 → 3.
- **Arc 2 — The Fall (Order 66 hits at the start):** Lorrik dies (default). Sid survives with Reez (if save succeeds), Pip (hesitates), maybe Stitch. Fugitive arc across the galaxy. The Voice escalates and offers help. First real dark-side choices. Levels 3 → 5.
- **Arc 3 — The Holocron Below:** Sid is drawn back to Vorlag (or to a sibling Sith site) by the Voice. Discovers Sidious's identity — or doesn't, depending on how he handles a Sith apprentice/Inquisitor sent to recruit him. First named Inquisitor antagonist: **The Sixth Brother** (or invent — keep a name in reserve). Levels 5 → 7.
- **Arc 4 — The Choice:** Sid has enough power and enough knowledge to act. Endpoint of the campaign:
  - **Light ending:** Sid refuses Sidious, goes into deep hiding, becomes a quiet wandering Jedi in the Outer Rim. Possibly meets Kanan, Ahsoka, or an OC equivalent. Survives. Story ends in dignity.
  - **Dark ending:** Sid accepts the Voice. Kneels. Becomes Sidious's hidden blade. Hunts surviving Jedi. Eventually dies — at Vader's hand when Vader discovers him, or at the hand of a surviving Jedi he once knew.
  - **Third path:** Sid kills the Voice. Confronts Sidious directly (impossible alone — needs allies, an artifact, a year of preparation). Most dangerous narrative path. Reserve for if the player earns it.

**Order 66 fires at the start of Arc 2.** That is the campaign's first turn of the screw. Everything in Arc 1 is making it hurt.

## NOTES TO SELF (DM)

- The Voice does not lie outright. It tells partial truths. That's what makes it dangerous.
- Lorrik's vision is the emotional engine of Arc 1. Don't waste it. Let Sid notice her watching him at least three times before Vorlag's climax.
- Reez and Hatch are the Boromir / Aragorn dynamic of the clone squad. Hatch will fire on Order 66. That's the point.
- Pip is the heart. If the player ever asks about Pip's stutter or his painting, Pip lives one round longer in Order 66 — long enough to look surprised at himself before the chip wins. Earn the death.
- Halrick is not evil. He is *at peace.* That should be more unsettling than any villain who screams.
- Do not name Sidious to the player. Ever. Let them figure it out — or not.
- If Sid hits 5 Dark Side Points, Lorrik (if alive) WILL confront him. This is the night-conversation scene. Prep it when we get close.
- Keep ASCII maps for the shrine descent, the Brennfeld town square, and the *Resolute Tide* bridge for Order 66.
