# Sid

**Race:** Human (Variant)
**Class:** Jedi Padawan (level 1)
**Background:** Acolyte (Jedi Temple, Coruscant)
**Alignment:** Lawful Good (drifting)
**Age:** 19
**XP:** 0 / 300

## Description

Tall and lean, dark hair cut short in the Padawan style with the braid worn over the right shoulder. Pale grey eyes that go very still when he's reading the Force. Carries himself with the calm of a knight long before he's earned it — the older Masters have noticed.

Trained at the Temple from infancy. Took to lightsaber forms faster than any Padawan in his cohort, but it's his Force sensitivity that's drawn quiet attention from the Council. He hears things in the Force others miss. Sometimes things that shouldn't be there at all.

## Class Features (Jedi Padawan)

- **Jedi Unarmored Defense:** AC = 10 + DEX + WIS while not wearing armor.
- **Force Sensitivity:** Advantage on Perception checks to detect living creatures within 30 ft, even through walls. Can sense Force users at greater range with focused concentration.
- **Lightsaber Mastery:** Proficient with lightsabers. Use DEX for attack and damage. Lightsabers ignore non-magical armor's AC bonus on a hit.
- **Lightsaber Forms:** Primary **Form V (Shien)** — counterattack-oriented, redirects blaster fire offensively. Secondary **Form IV (Ataru)** — acrobatic, fast, leaping strikes. Synergizes with Force Speed and Force Jump.
- **Force Powers Known (9):** Force Sense, Force Push, Force Pull, Force Jump, Force Speed, Telekinesis, Mind Trick, Saber Throw, Force Healing.

> **Exceptional Talent (campaign feature).** Sid's Force connection is unusually strong for his rank. The Council has noted it. Mechanically: +1 Force point above the feat baseline, and additional powers known beyond standard Padawan progression.

## Feat: Force-Sensitive (Variant Human)

Sid is unusually attuned to the Force, even for a Padawan.

- +2 Force points (already included in Force Pool above)
- Learn 1 additional Force power (Saber Throw — included)
- Once per long rest, ask the GM one yes/no question about a creature, location, or object you can sense. GM answers honestly with up to one word of context.

## Backstory (brief)

Found in the crèche records as a foundling from Corellia. Raised in the Temple. No memory of family. Took the saber crystal pilgrimage to Ilum at twelve, returned with a green crystal he hasn't spoken about since. Padawan to Master Tann Lorrik for the past three years — most of his master's later war career. He's seen things at her side that no one should have to. The Council expects him to be knighted within the year.

There is something he hasn't told anyone, not even his Master: in his meditations recently, he keeps hearing a voice he doesn't recognize, and he can't tell if it's in the Force or in his own head.
